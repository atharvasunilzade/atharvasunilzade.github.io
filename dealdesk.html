The part that matters in a dispute

Signatures built to survive "I never signed that"

A scanned signature proves almost nothing. Every DealDesk signature captures a tamper-evident evidence record instead.

Document fingerprint

A SHA-256 hash of the exact contract text is taken at the moment of signing. Change one character afterwards and the hash stops matching.

Explicit intent

The signer types their full legal name, draws a signature, confirms they've read it, and ticks an intent-to-be-legally-bound affirmation. Miss any one and signing is blocked.

Certificate of Completion

Every signed contract gets a certificate. If the document was altered after signing, it doesn't quietly pass — it flips to MISMATCH — document changed since signing.

Single-use signing links

Long, unguessable token URLs sent over WhatsApp or email. The link locks automatically once signed, and you can revoke or regenerate it whenever you want.

GPS proof of delivery

Schedule against an expected location, then verify on site — current GPS, computed distance from the target, recipient's name and signature.

Audit log

A chronological record of the key actions on every customer, quote, contract and invoice — so you can reconstruct what happened and when.

Questions we get asked

Before you switch from paper

Is an e-signature valid in India?

Electronic records and signatures are recognised under the Information Technology Act, 2000, and what matters in a dispute is the strength of your evidence. DealDesk is built to produce exactly that — fingerprint, typed legal name, explicit intent and a certificate. For agreements that require a Digital Signature Certificate or registration, take your own legal advice; we supply evidence, not legal advice.

Does my customer need an account?

No. They tap the link you send, sign on their own phone and they're done. No login, no app to install, nothing to remember.

What if I edit the contract afterwards?

The Certificate of Completion will say so. The fingerprint taken at signing won't match the changed text, and the certificate flips to a MISMATCH warning rather than passing quietly.

Do I need internet?

Only to send a remote signing link to someone else's phone. Quoting, invoicing, in-person signing and your records all run locally on your machine.

Can I use it for GST invoices?

Yes — line items, configurable default tax rate, discounts, totals, GSTIN on the customer record, and one-click conversion from quote to invoice with payment tracking.

Who is it for?

Anyone who quotes before they sell and delivers after they invoice — fabricators, contractors, equipment dealers, interior and civil work, service companies, and traders who want signed acceptance before goods move.
