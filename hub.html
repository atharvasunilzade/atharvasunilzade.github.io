What's inside

Billing, stock, ledgers, staff — in one place

Not a billing app bolted to a stock app bolted to an attendance app. One system, one database, one screen to learn.

GST billing & estimates

Estimates and GST invoices from the counter, payment tracking against each one, and running debit/credit balances per party.

Supplier bill scanning

Photograph an incoming bill and the Hub reads item names, quantities and printed rates, then makes the stock entry. It scores every reading and rejects any parse where quantity × rate can't explain the amount — so a bad scan is caught, not quietly filed.

WhatsApp delivery

Bills, receipts, refund vouchers, delivery notes and full statements of account go to the customer on WhatsApp. If the line is down, they queue and send themselves later.

Customer & vendor ledgers

Opening balances for old dues, manual entries, and a Tally-style statement of account for any party — viewable, printable and sendable.

Payment proof

Cash, UPI, PhonePe, NEFT, RTGS or cheque — each payment recorded with the amount, the balance before and after, a photo of the person and their signature.

Delivery proof

For goods that go out later by vehicle: delivery person, vehicle number, photo of the goods, photo of the person, signature, and an automatic delivery note to the customer.

Staff attendance & payroll

Face-recognition attendance with shifts, leave, overtime and early-logout approvals, feeding a payroll that pays against a confirmed identity.

Your data stays yours

Everything is also written out in plain, openable form — the full database plus Tally-style CSV ledgers for customers, vendors, products, estimates, payments and receipts.

Questions we get asked

Before you switch your billing

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The Hub runs on your own machine, so billing, stock and ledgers never stop. Anything meant for WhatsApp is queued and sent automatically once the connection is back — nothing is lost.

Do my staff have to learn a new app?

No, and that's deliberate. Shop-floor and delivery staff report on WhatsApp, including voice notes in Hindi and Marathi. Only you and your manager use the full screen.

Can it really read my supplier's bills?

Yes — including photos taken at an angle or sideways. It reads item names, quantities and the printed rate, and refuses any reading that doesn't add up arithmetically rather than entering something wrong.

Will I be locked in?

No. Alongside the working database, the Hub writes a plain readable copy and Tally-style CSV ledgers to a folder on your machine. You can open them in Excel any day, with or without us.

Can it be changed for my business?

That's the normal path. We tailor the Hub to how you already work — your bill format, your item names, your approval rules — and deliver it tested. We build custom software; this is our own.

What does it cost?

It depends on the edition and how much tailoring you need. Call or message us and we'll quote after a short demo run on your own bills and stock — not a generic slide deck.
