How we work

Research first. Code second. Live last.

Most trading software is written the wrong way round — the code gets built, then a backtest gets tuned until it looks good enough to fund. We do the opposite, and we are willing to reach the conclusion that an idea doesn't work.

  • Ranked by robustness, not by best-case return — we search across strategies and voting ensembles per instrument.
  • Fees and slippage modelled — a backtest that assumes perfect fills is a sales brochure, not evidence.
  • Per-trade ledgers you can open and audit, not a single equity curve you have to trust.
  • Paper mode by default — live order placement sits behind deliberate gates and hard risk limits.

Discover the edge

We test your hypothesis — and a systematic sweep of alternatives — across the instrument's history, and rank what survives.

Validate out-of-sample

The in-sample winners are re-run on data they never saw, then stress-tested with Monte-Carlo. Most ideas die here. That's the point.

Build it properly

Clean implementation, dashboard, trade log, risk limits and a kill switch — with paper trading as the default mode.

Hand it over

Source code, backtest reports and ledgers, documented. You own it and can run it without us.

The honest bit

We sell engineering, not predictions

Anyone promising you a strategy that makes money is either guessing or selling you something. We will not do either. Most ideas — including plenty of our own — fail out-of-sample testing, and our job is to find that out quickly and cheaply, before your capital does.

One of our own commodity desks carries a note in its documentation saying, in plain words, that no validated edge exists yet and nothing is armed. We would rather write that down than dress it up. You get the same treatment.

Questions we get asked

Before you send us an idea

Do you sell signals or tips?

No. We build software and hand you the source. We are not a registered adviser or research analyst, we don't tell you what to buy, and we don't trade for anyone. The decisions stay with you.

Will it be profitable?

We can't promise that and we won't. What we guarantee is a correct implementation and honest validation — including telling you when your idea doesn't survive, which is the most valuable thing we can find out early.

Can I bring my own strategy?

Yes, and that's the usual case. A rule you trade by hand, something from a book or a course, or a rough hypothesis — we'll code it exactly as specified and test it properly before you risk anything on it.

Which brokers and platforms?

MetaTrader 5 for FX, metals and CFDs, and Indian broker APIs for equities and MCX. Crypto and prediction markets over their public APIs. If you have a specific broker, tell us and we'll confirm before quoting.

Do you take a share of profits?

No. We charge for the engineering work, once. Profit-sharing would make us your fund manager, which we are not registered to be and have no intention of becoming.

How long does it take?

A single-instrument strategy with a backtest is usually a matter of weeks. A cross-asset research sweep with portfolio construction and live execution takes longer. We'll give you a timeline with the quote.

Tell us the market and the idea

Gold, silver, crypto, an NSE basket or something you've been trading by hand for years. Send it over and we'll tell you honestly whether it's worth testing.
