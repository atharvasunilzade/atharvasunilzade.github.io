How an engagement works

No surprises, no lock-in

We scope the work before quoting, build in reviewable stages so you can steer it, and hand over something you own outright. If you ever want to take it elsewhere, your code and your data go with you.

  • Fixed scope, quoted upfront — you know the number before we start.
  • Built in stages you can see — not a black box that appears months later.
  • Runs on your hardware where it matters — offline-first when the business can't wait on a connection.
  • Support that continues — updates, monitoring and fixes long after handover.
  • Your data stays exportable — plain files you can open without us.
  • Distance is not a problem — we work with clients in any country and any time zone.
A common starting point

"We already have software, but nobody uses it"

This is the most frequent conversation we have. A package was bought, the office staff were trained, and the people doing the actual work — on the shop floor, at the gate, in the delivery vehicle — never touched it. So the data was never entered, and within a month everyone was back to a register.

We design around that instead of against it. Structure lives where the manager sits. Reporting happens on the phone the worker already carries, in the language they already speak.
